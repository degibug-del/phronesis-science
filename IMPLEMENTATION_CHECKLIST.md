## Contingency Plans

### If IRB Says No
- **Reason likely**: Concern about subject burden or data handling
- **Response**: Add informed consent details, address privacy concerns, resubmit
- **Fallback**: Collaborate with another institution with IRB-approved study (borrow subjects)

### If First Paper Rejected
- **Reason likely**: Skepticism, methodological questions, weak effect size
- **Response**: Revise, address concerns, submit to backup journal
- **Timeline**: 1-2 month delay, not fatal

### If Collaborator Drops Out
- **Response**: Replace with next-best candidate from your list
- **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks to onboard new person

### If Funding Falls Through
- **Reason likely**: Tight competition, reviewer skepticism
- **Response**: Reduce team, focus on high-ROI experiments, resubmit to other sources
- **Timeline**: 3-6 month delay, not fatal

See FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md for full contingency planning.
