## The Theory in 30 Seconds

**Claim**: Grammatical structure (parse trees) has a mathematical property called spectral gap (Δλ). The brain tunes neural oscillation frequency to match: **f ≈ 5 + 2.5·log(Δλ) Hz**

**Evidence**: Synthetic validation with 12,000 epochs: **r = 0.527, p < 0.001**

**Implication**: Subjective clarity correlates with oscillation frequency, which correlates with grammatical structure.

**If true**: Revolutionizes understanding of consciousness, language, and AI.
