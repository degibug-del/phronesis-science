**Risk 1: Real data doesn't replicate synthetic results**

- Impact: Delay paradigm shift by 2-3 years, not fatal

- Mitigation: Large sample sizes, multi-site trials
- Fallback: Focus on research/technology rather than clinical
- Impact: Clinical applications delayed, other paths remain open

**Risk 3: Competing theory gains adoption**
- Mitigation: Publish aggressively, establish early priority
- Fallback: Integrate with other theories (not mutually exclusive)
- Impact: Reduced market share but not fatal

**Risk 4: Funding dries up**
- Mitigation: Diversify (NSF, NIH, private, industry)
- Fallback: Smaller team, focus on high-ROI experiments
- Impact: Slower progress but continues

**Risk 5: Key team member leaves**
- Mitigation: Build collaborative network, distribute knowledge
- Fallback: Recruit replacement (theory is attractive)
- Impact: Minor delays only
